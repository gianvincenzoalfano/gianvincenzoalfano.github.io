<h2 id="organization">Organization</h2>

- Program chair and organizing chair of the 7th Workshop on Advances in Argumentation in Artificial Intelligence (AI<sup>3</sup> 2023), co-located with AIxIA 2023, Rome, and editor of its proceedings ([CEUR-WS Vol. 3546](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-3546/)).
- Session chair at IJCAI-ECAI 2022, Knowledge Representation and Reasoning track, session on reasoning about knowledge and belief.
- Member of the organizing committee of the 29th Italian Symposium on Advanced Database Systems (SEBD 2021), Pizzo Calabro.
- Local organizer of the 13th International Conference on Flexible Query Answering Systems (FQAS 2019), Amantea.

<h2 id="editorial">Editorial activity</h2>

- Review editor, Data Mining and Management section of *Frontiers in Big Data*, since July 2021.
- Guest editor, with Guillermo Ricardo Simari and Irina Trubitsyna, of the special issue [Argumentation-based Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/intelligent-systems-with-applications/special-issue/106MPXF6W1V) of *Intelligent Systems with Applications*.
- Guest editor, with Alejandro J. García and Francesco Parisi, of the special issue Knowledge Representation Formalisms for AI Applications of *Big Data and Cognitive Computing*.

Conferences, besides those listed above: ACL Rolling Review (2026), CLAR 2021, COMMA 2020, ICLP 2020, ParLearning 2017.

Silver Tier reviewer distinction, Primary Paper Initiative of IJCAI-ECAI 2026, awarded by the program chair for timely, substantive reviews and active participation in the discussion phase.

<h2 id="institutional">Institutional roles</h2>

- Member of the Board of Professors (Collegio dei Docenti) of the PhD programme in Information and Communication Technologies, DIMES, since November 2025.
- Member of the DIMES Department Board (Giunta di Dipartimento) as representative of the researchers, April 2024 to January 2026.
- Member of the joint council of the Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Computer Engineering (DIMES) since 2023/2024, and of those in Management Engineering (DIMEG) from 2020/2021 to 2022/2023.
- Member of the examining board for the professional engineering qualification (Esame di Stato), computer science area, sessions I and II of 2025.
- Secretary of the committee evaluating the proposals of the PNRR FAIR cascade call, objective “Abstract Argumentation for Knowledge Representation and Reasoning”.
- Member, usually as secretary, of selection committees for teaching assignments, tutoring positions, research grants and fellowships at DIMES (2022–2026).
